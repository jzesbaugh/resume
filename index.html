Published

Blind Evaluation Protocol for LLM Instruction Sets

I write the rules my AI assistant runs on, then test whether they actually change anything. Pass and fail are written down before a run, questions get retired once they've been used, and — the part that matters — the same questions are asked again with no rules at all, as a control. That comparison is the only way to tell a rule that works from a rule that was never needed, and it showed several of mine were not earning their place. The system also restocks itself: when something goes wrong in real work it becomes a candidate rule, gets tested against the control, and is kept or dropped on the result. Twice now the testing has proved one of my own predictions wrong. Published, not yet adopted by anyone else. Source
